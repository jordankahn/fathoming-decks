Refresher · Your turn

Draw. Charge. Play. End.

i

Draw

Draw 1 card.

ii

Energy

Start at 4. One more for each turn you have finished, up to 10.

iii

Play, in any order

  • Summon Warriors
  • Play a Terrain
  • Play Actions, equip Items
  • Use Activate abilities
  • Attack: once per Warrior
  • Pay 2 Energy to draw a card
iv

End

End-of-turn effects happen. Unspent Energy is gone.

Energy does not carry over. Both players start on 4 and climb by one each turn to a cap of 10. In the play phase you can do things in any order, and each Warrior attacks once. Don't forget you can always pay 2 Energy to draw.

Rule updates

What has changed since you last played

November 2026Divine discount doubled

A Divine card costs 10, minus 1→2 for each Loot you are behind. Three behind, it costs 4. Five behind, it is free. Tied or ahead, still 10.

April 2026Empty field penalty

End your turn with no Warriors and your opponent takes 2 Loot→1 Loot. This applies from your second turn onward.

Two changes since most of you last played. The big one is new this week: Divine cards now drop by 2 Energy for every Loot you trail by, so comebacks arrive much faster. The other: an empty field now costs you 1 Loot, not 2.
Rule updates · Rulings

Rulings worth knowing

1

No target, no play

An Action with nothing it can legally target cannot be played. Hidden zones don't count: you may still play a search that turns up nothing.

2

“Up to” includes none

Whenever a card says “up to”, choosing nothing at all is allowed.

3

Actions discard last

An Action is not in your discard pile until it has finished. It can never fetch itself back.

4

Who beats whom

When effects clash: Global Terrain, then Personal Terrain, then Warriors and their Items, then Actions. The later one wins.

5

Over the Warrior limit

If an effect shrinks your field, you choose who goes. They are discarded, so no Loot changes hands.

6

Searches end in a shuffle

Show your opponent what you found, then shuffle, unless the card says otherwise.

7

Full Arts share the limit

A Full Art and its regular version count as the same card: 3 copies between them.

8

Costs stop at zero

No Summon Cost or Attack Cost can ever drop below 0.

These are calls that have come up in play over the last year. Number one matters most in practice: you cannot throw away an Action that has no legal target.

New Mechanics

Shrines of Sin, and the Warriors who serve them.

Two new ideas carry the set: Shrines, and the Servants bound to them.
New mechanic

Shrines of Sin

1

A new class of Terrain: Sin. Each costs 4, with 130 Health.

2

Only playable in your Personal Terrain zone. Never Global.

3

Each grants a Blessing and demands a Punishment.

4

One Personal Terrain, so one Shrine at a time.

Shrines are Terrains with a catch. They only go in your own personal zone, so your opponent can't share them, and every Shrine pays you with one hand and takes with the other.
New mechanic

Servants of Sin

Some Warriors are corrupted. They still belong to a normal class, but they are sworn to one Shrine. Look for the mark under the name.

Their Shrine is in your personal zone

The Servant's ability works, and the Shrine's Blessing and Punishment apply to it.

No matching Shrine

The Servant has no ability. It still fights with its printed stats.

If the Shrine leaves play, all of it switches off at once. “Corrupted warrior” on a card means any Servant of Sin.

This is the rule to remember tonight, because it is not printed on the cards. A Servant's ability only works while its own Shrine is sitting in your personal terrain zone. Lose the Shrine and your Servants are just bodies.
Sinful Shadows · The fine print

How the new cards are ruled

1

Personal zone only

A Shrine only ever sits in your own Personal Terrain zone, never the Global one.

2

No Shrine, no ability

A Servant's ability is off unless its own Shrine is in your personal zone. Someone else's Shrine does not count.

3

Everything falls together

When a Shrine leaves play, its Blessing, its Punishment and its Servants' abilities all stop immediately.

4

Envy and Pride

Their Punishment only switches off the Shrine's Blessing. Their Servants keep their own abilities.

5

Mr. Egg silences Servants too

While Mr. Egg disables the Shrines, their Servants go dormant with them.

6

Stolen abilities really move

When The Job Siphon or Boo Boo the Fool takes an ability, the Warrior it came from is left with none.

A few calls on the new cards so nobody has to argue about them at the table.

Sinful Shadows · Divine

Three new Divine cards

Smelling Pancakes is the first card you can play on your opponent's turn.

When they are about to take the Loot that wins them the game, you may play it. They take none of it.

It costs nothing to play. It is still a Divine card, so it is the only one your deck can hold.

Smelling Pancakes breaks the one rule the game has always had: you only act on your own turn. It cancels a game-winning Loot grab. It costs 0, but one Divine card per deck means choosing it gives up the others.
